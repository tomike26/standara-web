THE TRUST SCORE

Proven past delivery: the best guide to future delivery

A 0–100 score in five bands, from Limited to Exceptional. Explainable, challengeable, and never a ranking. Each score comes with a recommended contract value: a lower band says “start smaller”, never “no”.

0 · LIMITEDEXCEPTIONAL · 100
  • Company age is never used
  • New suppliers get a provisional score, capped at 75
  • Suppliers see their score and can challenge it

THE STANDARA ASSURANCE SCALE

Every piece of evidence shows how far you can rely on it

ASSURANCE 1

Self-reported

The supplier says it was done. Nothing attached.

ASSURANCE 2

Document-backed

Backed by the supplier's own documents or systems: photos, timesheets, certificates.

ASSURANCE 3

Independently corroborated

Confirmed by someone outside the supplier: the buyer, a delivery partner or an official register.

Published openly. Buyers can require Assurance 3 before an invoice is paid.
