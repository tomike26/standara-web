THE PROBLEM

Supplier delivery is invisible. So buyers award blind and lenders lend blind.

01

Award blind

Buyers choose suppliers on self-declared claims, questionnaires and references. Nobody checks what was actually delivered.

02

Pay on trust

Contract managers chase monthly returns, then take them on trust. Invoices are paid with little link to proof.

03

Proof that goes nowhere

A supplier that delivers well cannot carry that proof to its next bid, or borrow against it.

HOW IT WORKS

Proven delivery becomes a record. The record becomes a Trust Score.

  1. STEP 1

    Deliver

    The supplier records delivery and submits evidence against each contract commitment.

  2. STEP 2

    Corroborate

    The buyer, a delivery partner or a data feed confirms it independently.

  3. STEP 3

    Record

    The confirmed outcome becomes a Verified Contract Record in the supplier's history.

  4. STEP 4

    Score

    Every record feeds a 0–100 Trust Score the supplier can see and challenge.

  5. STEP 5

    Trusted

    Buyers check delivery history before award. Lenders fund on the score.

WHY NOW

Buyers must now prove delivery. Nobody holds the evidence.

New rules require public and private buyers to record, publish and act on supplier performance. Standara holds the evidence they need.

  1. Feb 2025Procurement Act 2023 in force: KPIs, published performance, exclusion for poor delivery
